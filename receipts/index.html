Scan any receipt and Receipts tracks your return windows and warranty expiry, then reminds you before the deadline passes.

What it does

⏰

Get reminded before a return window closes or a warranty runs out, so you never miss your chance to get money back or a repair.

🔎

Search every receipt by store, item, or amount — even text printed inside the receipt body.

🧾

Claim helper

Keep the receipt, invoice, product photo and serial number together, ready to attach when you file a warranty claim.

Your receipts are stored on your device and locked behind Face ID. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Your language
